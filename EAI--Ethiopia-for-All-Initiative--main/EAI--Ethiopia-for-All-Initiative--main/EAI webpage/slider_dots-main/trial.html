Ethiopia for All Initiative.

Ethiopia for All Initiative (EAI) is a local community based non-governmental organization working towards leading the way to achieve lasting development through community engagement, women empowermentthrough collaboration and working with partners from different sectors.

EAI and what it does

Join Your Helping Hands And Assist Us In Making a Difference In This World

MISSION

To alleviate poverty in all its forms through community engagement, capacity building, and policy-based advocacy to address barriers facing vulnerable and at-risk women and girls, youth, elder, and people with diversity & inclusion.

VISSION

We aspire to see an economically strong and socially vibrant country where its strength comes from its society’s high productivity and interdependence.

    • Organizational Background

    EAI was established in March 2020 with a mission to work to better the lives of those living in extreme poverty in order to empower them and provide them with access to basic health care, education, and clean water. EAI's work also includes institutional capacity building in a variety of areas. In its long-term strategy, EAI will raise awareness about the effects of climate change on the environment and what mitigation measures may be performed at the community level to keep our ecosystem safe. EAI is dedicated to the principles of diversity and inclusion, as well as advocating for those who are marginalized.

    How And Where Does The Donations Go

    We aims to empower impoverished women and girls by equipping them with entrepreneurial skills and connecting them to essential resources for achieving socioeconomic independence. In addition, it provides critical social welfare support to vulnerable groups, including destitute elderly, street children, and individuals with severe disabilities.

    Our Objectives

    • To equip impoverished women and girls with entrepreneurial skills and connect them with the necessary resources to gain socioeconomic independence
      To provide social welfare to destitute elderly, children on the street, and people with severe disabilities.
      To increase public awareness of diseases associated with increasingly urbanized and modern lifestyles.
      To support increased access to quality education with a primary focus on reducing and eliminating financial barriers for girls
      To increase access to clean and safe water/sanitation facilities
      To collaborate with various actors such as government to promote diversity and inclusion through hosting capacity building trainings, providing consultations on related policies, and engaging in research on diversity and inclusion
      To promote public awareness of climate change, and provide education on the knowledge, skills, and technologies necessary to support adaptation
